New to pace? Pace means the time you take to run one kilometre. For example, 8:00 min/km means 8 minutes per kilometre. A larger number means a slower pace. Walking breaks are welcome on the first run.

Here’s what to expect when you arrive.

  1. Meet 10 minutes before the start

    Your run details show the exact meeting point and how to recognise your host.

  2. Say hello — coming alone is welcome

    The host introduces the group and explains the route. Bring water and comfortable shoes.

  3. Move at a pace that fits

    Check each run’s pace and rest policy. Our beginner run includes walking breaks and regrouping.

Regular running can improve cardiovascular fitness. Start at a level that suits you and build gradually.

Being active can support mood, sleep and mental wellbeing. You do not need to chase a personal best.
