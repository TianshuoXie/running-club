Ideas considered

Better activity information was selected because users need to judge suitability before joining. A buddy-matching feature was deferred because it needs real profiles and privacy decisions. A browser-local conversation board was added after feedback about newcomers, progress and activity benefits. Live chat remains outside this prototype.
